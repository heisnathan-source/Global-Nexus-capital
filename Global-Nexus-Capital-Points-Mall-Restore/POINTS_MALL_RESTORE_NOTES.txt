Restore for the current Global Nexus Capital repository.

Files:
  app/points-mall/page.jsx

The page restores the original Points Shop behavior:
- two-column shop grid
- clickable product cards
- Redeem Item confirmation
- POST /api/points/purchase
- balance update after purchase
- purchase history
- points history
- congratulations screen
- contact-admin prompt

The supplied page expects the existing /api/points and /api/points/purchase routes; it does not alter the database or purchase API.

The CSS block in apply-points-mall-restore.sh adds the two-column layout, clickable cards, modal and celebration styling. It also adds rotating card colors for the visible shop items.
